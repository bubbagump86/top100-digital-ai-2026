What this is

AI startup headlines usually mix private valuations with public-market caps, and conflate revenue with projections, with hardware, and with services. This site isolates the pure-play digital AI business — foundation models, AI applications, agent platforms, AI coding, voice and video gen, AI-native data, etc. — and ranks them with one transparent scoring rule.
