# Company Category Valuation AI ARR Score

How is the score built?

Each company's valuation and AI-attributed ARR are first log-transformed, then min-max rescaled to [0,1] across the surviving set. The composite score is 100 × (0.5 × z_val + 0.5 × z_arr).

For diversified companies (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, Salesforce, ServiceNow) the valuation is derived: AI-attributed ARR × the pure-play median EV/Revenue multiple. For companies with no AI-revenue disclosure, the full market cap is used as a flagged proxy. See the methodology page, sections 3 and 6b.

Valuation cap. A derived AI valuation can never exceed the actual valuation of the company it belongs to — a part cannot be worth more than the whole — so the axis is MIN(AI ARR × multiple, market cap). Where the cap binds, the uncapped figure is shown next to it and the row is badged. One row binds in the 2026 set: Baidu.

Score is sensitive to the most recent disclosed valuation and ARR figures, which are reported across multiple dated press cycles. Rerank with any updated numbers by re-running scripts/score.py.
