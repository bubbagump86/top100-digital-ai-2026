# Company Category Valuation ARR Score

How is the score built?

Each company's valuation and ARR are first log-transformed, then min-max rescaled to [0,1] across the surviving set. The composite score is 100 × (0.5 × z_val + 0.5 × z_arr). See the methodology page for the full treatment, including missing-data rules and edge cases.

Score is sensitive to the most recent disclosed valuation and ARR figures, which are reported across multiple dated press cycles. Rerank with any updated numbers by re-running scripts/score.py.
